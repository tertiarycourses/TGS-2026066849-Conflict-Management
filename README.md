## About the course

Workplace conflict is an inevitable reality in every organisation. This course builds a practical foundation in conflict management: recognising conflict types and escalation stages, selecting a management style, de-escalating difficult conversations with active listening and reframing, negotiating on interests, mediating to an actionable agreement, and embedding guidelines and leadership practices that prevent recurrence. More than half of the training time is role play and case work on fictional Singapore workplace scenarios.

## Learning outcomes

1. Analyse workplace conflict types, escalation stages, and management styles to select appropriate conflict resolution approaches for organisational settings.
2. Apply active listening, empathy, reframing, and non-verbal communication techniques to de-escalate and manage conflict conversations effectively.
3. Apply interest-based negotiation and consensus-building techniques to facilitate collaborative problem-solving and overcome resistance among conflicting parties.
4. Evaluate and apply structured conflict resolution and mediation frameworks to develop actionable plans aligned with organisational goals.
5. Develop organisational guidelines and leadership strategies to prevent recurring conflicts and embed constructive conflict management practices.

## Topics covered

1. **Understanding Workplace Conflict and Management Styles** — conflict types, escalation cycle, warning signs, Thomas-Kilmann modes, situational selection
2. **Communication Techniques for Diffusing Tensions** — active listening, empathy, reframing, neutral language, non-verbal cues, self-regulation
3. **Negotiation and Consensus-Building Strategies** — positions and interests, BATNA, reservation points, ZOPA, option generation, resistance, consensus testing
4. **Achieving Resolution and Protecting Organisational Interests** — structured resolution, mediation stages, impartiality, option evaluation, action planning
5. **Sustaining a Conflict-Resilient Organisation** — guidelines, escalation protocols, psychological safety, root-cause review, monitoring
